Feedback and corrections

Contact guidance

Data Storage Lab is in its first public implementation phase. A project contact address has not yet been confirmed, so this page does not publish an unmonitored email address or a nonfunctional form.

Current contact status

There is no confirmed public contact method for this project yet. When one is established, this page will be updated with a monitored route and the privacy policy will be revised to match.

Useful feedback to prepare

If you later submit a correction, include the page URL, your inputs, the result you saw, the unit convention you expected, and the specific claim or calculation you believe needs review. Do not include passwords, recovery keys, account credentials, or private files.

What this page will not collect

The planner is designed to calculate from values entered in the browser. It does not ask you to upload your documents, photos, drive serial numbers, or backup contents.

For privacy and analytics information, see the Privacy Policy.
