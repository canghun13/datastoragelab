Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026. This page describes the information practices for the current Data Storage Lab site implementation.

Planner values

The Home Storage & Backup Planner calculates from the values you enter in your browser. The current implementation does not provide accounts, file uploads, or a server-side planner database. Do not enter passwords, recovery keys, confidential client information, or sensitive personal data.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics 4 with measurement ID G-Z7QV39WJ35 to understand general site use. Google may collect information through cookies or similar technologies according to its own policies and your browser or consent settings.

Cookies and similar technologies

Analytics and future advertising or affiliate integrations may use cookies, local storage, pixels, or similar technologies. If the site adopts a consent-management approach or changes tracking behavior, this policy will be updated to describe the actual implementation.

Advertising and affiliate links

The current Phase 1 pages do not include ad code or affiliate links. Future pages may display advertising or use clearly disclosed affiliate links. Those relationships will not change planner calculations or vendor-neutral recommendations.

External services

Links to external websites and services are governed by their own privacy practices. Data Storage Lab is hosted through services used for static site delivery and may rely on their normal operational logs.

Data retention and security

Because the current planner does not create visitor accounts or collect uploaded planning records, Data Storage Lab does not maintain a user-facing storage database for those inputs. No internet service can promise absolute security; use care when sharing information through any future contact method.

Contact

A public contact method is not yet confirmed. See Contact guidance for the current status. When a monitored contact route is published, this policy will be updated.
