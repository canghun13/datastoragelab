NAS vs Cloud for Family Photos

Choose an architecture from access, recovery, privacy, capacity, and independent backup requirements—not a product ranking.

NAS

A NAS can provide local control and fast home access, but it still needs independent backup copies and a site-resilience plan.

Cloud

A cloud service can simplify offsite access, but recovery speed, recurring cost tolerance, and account recovery need consideration.

Hybrid

A local primary with an offsite component can combine fast access with separation from a home-site event.

Decision checklist

Measure data, define recovery expectations, identify privacy and control requirements, then create an independent-copy plan. Use the topology selector and Home Storage & Backup Planner.
