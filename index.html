What we do not do

We do not start with brands, treat RAID as backup, or pretend a single formula is a complete storage plan.

RAID helps availability; independent backups protect recoverability.

A mirrored or parity-protected array can reduce downtime after a drive failure. It does not create an independent copy for deletion, ransomware, theft, fire, or site-wide failure.
