Local Backup vs Offsite Backup

Local and offsite copies solve different recovery problems; most important data benefits from both.

Different jobs

Local copies can restore large amounts of data quickly. Offsite copies can remain available after theft, fire, or a site-wide event. Neither is automatically enough alone.

Choose a combined pattern

Keep a separately stored local recovery target and an offsite copy of the data that needs site resilience. Check whether upload and restore time are acceptable.
