How to use the Guides hub

Where to start

Start with the guide that explains the assumption blocking your decision. How Much NAS Storage covers capacity framing; RAID Is Not a Backup and 3-2-1 explain protection roles; retention, snapshots, and local versus offsite clarify recovery policy. HDD versus SSD and CMR versus SMR address media choices, while drive replacement and UPS sizing cover operation.

Recommended workflow

Read one concept guide, return to the matching calculator with better evidence, and save the revised result. Follow internal links only when they answer the next unresolved question. Capacity normally precedes configuration, configuration precedes backup topology, recovery requirements precede network and cost, and operating practices follow procurement. This prevents an appealing technology term from becoming the plan’s starting assumption.

What to prepare

Bring the decision you need to make, the current tool output, and the assumptions you distrust. Note whether the issue concerns usable capacity, failure tolerance, independent copies, retention, transfer time, media workload, replacement, or power protection. Guides explain tradeoffs and terminology; they do not replace current vendor documentation, measured workloads, or specialist advice for regulated systems.

What to do next

After reading, use the linked tool to quantify the concept and the reference library to verify units or formulas. Compare pages are useful when two complete approaches remain plausible. Record what changed in your plan after the guide; if no input, constraint, or action changed, identify the remaining evidence gap instead of browsing indefinitely.
