HDD recording methods

CMR vs SMR for NAS

Make recording method a documented part of drive selection when arrays, rebuilds, or sustained writes matter.

The concept

CMR and SMR are recording approaches used by hard drives. They are not universal quality grades. Their suitability depends on the write pattern, rebuild exposure, and operational expectations of your specific workload.

Common mistake

Do not infer recording method from a drive family name, capacity, or retailer listing. Check current manufacturer documentation for the exact model and record the evidence in the purchase brief.

Decision rules

  • For arrays, rebuild-sensitive systems, or sustained/random writes, specify CMR.
  • For a light, documented single-drive archive pattern, SMR may be conditionally suitable.
  • When recording method is uncertain, treat it as an unresolved compatibility requirement.

Checklist

  1. Describe the write and rebuild workload.
  2. Run the CMR vs SMR Suitability Checker.
  3. Verify current manufacturer documentation.
  4. Confirm independent backup and restore testing.

Limits

Recording method does not tell you a drive’s capacity, vibration tolerance, warranty, firmware behavior, or suitability in a specific enclosure. Confirm each requirement separately.
