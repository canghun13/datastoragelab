How the calculation or recommendation works

Transfer hours equal TB times eight million megabits divided by effective Mbps and 3,600 seconds. The estimator adds setup and verification minutes to produce an end-to-end planning duration. The Recovery Time Estimator keeps restore duration arithmetic transparent, allowing the recovery-time estimate to be checked without a vendor account. In Recovery Time Estimator, follow every restore size, throughput, and procedure unit shown by Recovery Time Estimator. Decimal TB and TiB answer capacity questions. Keep every Recovery Time Estimator input in its displayed unit; preserve those Recovery Time Estimator labels when sharing or checking the result. Round the recovery-time estimate upward only after reviewing the unrounded Recovery Time Estimator result.

How to interpret the results

Separate transfer time from procedural delay. If the combined result exceeds the recovery-time objective, improving only the network may not help when source media, destination writes, account access, or validation is slower. Read the Recovery Time Estimator summary with its restore duration metrics. Use the Recovery Time Estimator table and caution note to examine the complete restore size, throughput, and procedure tradeoff. Save effective restore speed inputs with the result, compare scenarios under the same restore duration definition, and investigate conflicting recovery-time estimate output before acting.

Worked example

Restoring 4 TB at an effective 100 Mbps takes about 88.9 transfer hours. Adding sixty minutes of setup and verification produces an end-to-end estimate near 89.9 hours—far beyond a same-day target. Assumptions and limitations

Throughput can change with small files, encryption, decompression, cloud throttles, media handling, contention, and damaged data. The formula assumes a sustained effective rate and a known restore procedure. The Recovery Time Estimator is a planning aid for restore size, throughput, and procedure in Recovery Time Estimator. Treat the restore duration assumptions as boundaries: the recovery-time estimate supports restore duration planning only. It does not certify an implementation of Recovery Time Estimator, guarantee recovery, or provide financial advice. Recheck effective restore speed, documentation relevant to Recovery Time Estimator, and operating requirements before purchasing for Recovery Time Estimator, migrating data, or approving a policy.
