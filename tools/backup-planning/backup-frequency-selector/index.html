How the calculation or recommendation works

An interval at or below one hour suggests continuous or hourly protection; up to twenty-four hours suggests daily automation; longer intervals suggest weekly scheduling supplemented by event-triggered backups. The Backup Frequency Selector keeps recovery-point schedule arithmetic transparent, allowing the backup cadence to be checked without a vendor account. In Backup Frequency Selector, follow every acceptable loss and change rate unit shown by Backup Frequency Selector. Decimal TB and TiB answer capacity questions. Keep every Backup Frequency Selector input in its displayed unit; preserve those Backup Frequency Selector labels when sharing or checking the result. Round the backup cadence upward only after reviewing the unrounded Backup Frequency Selector result.

Assumptions and limitations

A simple threshold cannot model application consistency, open files, database logs, device availability, job duration, legal retention, bandwidth, or criticality differences inside the 4 TB scope. It does not certify an implementation of Backup Frequency Selector, guarantee recovery, or provide financial advice.
