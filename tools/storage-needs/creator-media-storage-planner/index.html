Implementation note

Plan for the whole workflow

Media production often needs a fast workspace, durable archive capacity, and a separately recoverable copy. This planner makes those bands visible so a fast scratch tier is not mistaken for an archive.

Example

A creator ingesting 120 GB on 30 shooting days begins with roughly 3.6 TB of annual material. Growth, archive copies, and reserve can make the total allocation far larger than the current active project.
