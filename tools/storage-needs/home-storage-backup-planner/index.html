    How this recommendation works

    Assumptions you can inspect and adjust

    The planner grows your current decimal-TB data by the annual rate over the selected period, adds expansion headroom, then tests a same-size drive layout with the protection level you selected. Backup capacity is estimated separately from the important-data share, retention level, local copies, and optional offsite copy.

    Transfer time assumes 75% of the entered upload speed. Cost is a broad planning range based on capacity class and equipment categories, not a live product quote. Mixed drive sizes, vendor-specific RAID behavior, data compression, and actual UPS runtime need separate confirmation before purchase.

    How to use the result

    1. Adjust any assumption that feels optimistic or unclear.
    2. Use the configuration as a specification brief: bays, drive count, minimum drive size, protection, backup capacity, network tier, and UPS class.
    3. Confirm compatible layouts, drive recording method, power/runtime figures, and software support with the vendors you evaluate.
    4. Set a reminder to revisit the plan before available usable capacity falls below 20%.

    Decision guide

    Use Home Storage & Backup Planner with confidence

    This guide explains the household data map decision behind Home Storage & Backup Planner, the evidence worth collecting for capacity and recovery, and the boundaries of its purchase brief.     Before you start

    Inventory current decimal terabytes, annual growth, planning years, expansion headroom, protected devices, important-data share, retention, independent copies, upload speed, LAN tier, failure tolerance, and budget posture. For Home Storage & Backup Planner, write the household data map assumption beside every saved result. That purchase brief record lets another person reproduce the capacity and recovery scenario, challenge future household growth, and rerun Home Storage & Backup Planner after conditions change. Test one conservative household data map case and one demanding case, then compare their purchase brief range.

    How the calculation or recommendation works

    The planner compounds today’s data through the selected horizon, adds headroom, sizes a protected same-drive array, applies retention overhead to important data, and estimates local and offsite copy capacity, transfer timing, network posture, and a broad budget band. The Home Storage & Backup Planner keeps household data map arithmetic transparent, allowing the purchase brief to be checked without a vendor account. In Home Storage & Backup Planner, follow every capacity and recovery unit shown by Home Storage & Backup Planner. Decimal TB and TiB answer capacity questions. Keep every Home Storage & Backup Planner input in its displayed unit; preserve those Home Storage & Backup Planner labels when sharing or checking the result. Round the purchase brief upward only after reviewing the unrounded Home Storage & Backup Planner result.

    How to interpret the results

    Start with projected usable capacity and the suggested bay layout, then compare local-backup and offsite allocations. Treat the upload estimate as a feasibility warning: a long seed time may justify staged upload, faster service, or a local recovery copy. Read the Home Storage & Backup Planner summary with its household data map metrics. Use the Home Storage & Backup Planner table and caution note to examine the complete capacity and recovery tradeoff. Save future household growth inputs with the result, compare scenarios under the same household data map definition, and investigate conflicting purchase brief output before acting.

    Assumptions and limitations

    The budget is a planning band rather than a live quotation. Generic array sizing cannot confirm a NAS vendor’s expansion rules, mixed-drive behavior, filesystem reserve, deduplication, compression, taxes, service throttles, or recovery guarantees. The Home Storage & Backup Planner is a planning aid for capacity and recovery in Home Storage & Backup Planner. Treat the household data map assumptions as boundaries: the purchase brief supports household data map planning only. It does not certify an implementation of Home Storage & Backup Planner, guarantee recovery, or provide financial advice. Recheck future household growth, documentation relevant to Home Storage & Backup Planner, and operating requirements before purchasing for Home Storage & Backup Planner, migrating data, or approving a policy.
