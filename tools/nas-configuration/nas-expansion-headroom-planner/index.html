Why use an 80% trigger

Capacity close to full leaves too little room for normal operations, recovery, and schedule flexibility. This tool marks 80% of current usable capacity as an early planning trigger, not a hard technical limit.

How the calculation or recommendation works

The model compounds current data until it reaches 80% of usable capacity, up to twenty years. Free-bay addition is reported as raw drive capacity only, because protection rules determine how much becomes usable. The NAS Expansion Headroom Planner keeps expansion trigger arithmetic transparent, allowing the capacity runway to be checked without a vendor account. In NAS Expansion Headroom Planner, follow every growth and free-bay runway unit shown by NAS Expansion Headroom Planner. Decimal TB and TiB answer capacity questions. Keep every NAS Expansion Headroom Planner input in its displayed unit; preserve those NAS Expansion Headroom Planner labels when sharing or checking the result. Round the capacity runway upward only after reviewing the unrounded NAS Expansion Headroom Planner result.

How to interpret the results

The review year is an action threshold rather than an exhaustion date. Free-bay raw addition shows physical potential; a zero-bay result signals that replacement, a second volume, or migration planning must begin earlier. Read the NAS Expansion Headroom Planner summary with its expansion trigger metrics. Use the NAS Expansion Headroom Planner table and caution note to examine the complete growth and free-bay runway tradeoff. Save compounded consumption inputs with the result, compare scenarios under the same expansion trigger definition, and investigate conflicting capacity runway output before acting.

Assumptions and limitations

Growth may be uneven, and NAS platforms differ on online expansion, drive order, mixed sizes, volume limits, and reshape risk. The tool does not estimate migration time or temporary backup space. The NAS Expansion Headroom Planner is a planning aid for growth and free-bay runway in NAS Expansion Headroom Planner. Treat the expansion trigger assumptions as boundaries: the capacity runway supports expansion trigger planning only. It does not certify an implementation of NAS Expansion Headroom Planner, guarantee recovery, or provide financial advice. Recheck compounded consumption, documentation relevant to NAS Expansion Headroom Planner, and operating requirements before purchasing for NAS Expansion Headroom Planner, migrating data, or approving a policy.
