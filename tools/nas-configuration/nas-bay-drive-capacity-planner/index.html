How layouts are calculated

The tool applies your reserve to required usable decimal TB, then subtracts the number of drives used for the selected generic protection model. It does not assume that all NAS operating systems expand or protect arrays identically.

Use the result

Choose a feasible bay class, verify its supported RAID layouts, and validate the selected drive count with the RAID Capacity Calculator. See Storage and RAID Capacity Formulas for the math and limits.
